- [Lecture 24, lines 38977–39061 and 39108–39226](../../assets/f26-subtitles.txt#L38977) gives the renamed file with the same hash, the student objection that a hash does not indicate a location, the overlay, and DHT circle. - Freshness, conditional GET, and the versioned manifest example extend the lectures with [RFC 9111, §§4–5](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9111.html#section-4) and [RFC 9110, §§13.1.2 and 15.4.5](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9110.html#section-13.1.2). The deterministic lab is new. It drops expired versions and models a full body fetch on misses. It deliberately omits revalidation, concurrent prefetching and queued I/O so the trace isolates storage decisions. The shared-dependency scenario and lead-in to Chapter 21 are new.
