# Chapter 14 source notes

- [Lecture 16, lines 24808–25048](../../assets/f26-subtitles.txt#L24808) starts from the signal/noise model and develops the static spectrum assignment proposal, its 50-device example, and the student questions about idle bands and joining. The 51st device and outage scenario are new.
- [Lecture 16, lines 25334–25575](../../assets/f26-subtitles.txt#L25334) uses conversations, lowering power over shorter distances, directional antennas, and dynamic frequency grants. It discusses cellular scheduling as an example of allocating time-frequency resources and the need for a way to send requests. No numerical assertion about antenna count or allowed frequencies is carried over.
- [Lecture 16, lines 25861–26102](../../assets/f26-subtitles.txt#L25861) uses raising hands, the corridor hidden sender example, a second access point created by turning on a phone hotspot, and coloring a graph of neighboring cells. The exposed terminal counterexample, cumulative-interference counterexample, and receiver observations are new. Exact assignments and coexistence procedures vary by system.
- [Lecture 16, lines 26225–26573](../../assets/f26-subtitles.txt#L26225) derives carrier sensing, the hallway symmetry example, random attempts at probability 1/3, the lottery analogy for fairness, the n-contender formula, ACK learning, and RTS/CTS. The link-level ACK detector cannot establish cause. The connection to standardized DCF behavior is checked against [Bianchi, §II](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/840210/). The planned Lecture 16 InstaPoll was replaced by open feedback, [lines 26564–26580](../../assets/f26-subtitles.txt#L26564).
- Rendered and inspected [Quiz 2, 2024, Q2–Q3, pp. 2–5](../../assets/cs356-quiz2-2024.pdf#page=2), including the wall layout, normalized powers and frequency/time alternatives. The book uses the exact Shannon form from Chapter 13, with linear SNR and SINR. [Q8, p. 8](../../assets/cs356-quiz2-2024.pdf#page=8) motivates the ten-contender experiment. The model samples independent attempts with saturated demand and equal-length slots.
- Rendered and inspected [Quiz 2, 2025, Q1, p. 1](../../assets/quiz2-2025.pdf#page=1). The quiz compares throughput and latency for central time-and-frequency allocation with a T-second horizon, explicitly granting broadcast reliability. The halved horizon and worked examples are new.
- [Lecture 21, lines 31914–32025](../../assets/f26-subtitles.txt#L31914) returns to the air-time question that was intended as an InstaPoll and derives 60p = 6(1−p), equal resources, and throughput maximization. Rendered and inspected [Quiz 2, 2026, Q2, pp. 2–3](../../assets/quiz2-2026.pdf#page=2), including the diagram and Bob's mobility question. Chapter 12 already treats equal throughput versus airtime in detail. The packet-level airtime tag adaptation, Bob's 4 Mbit/s requirement, and the treatment of retransmission charges are new.
