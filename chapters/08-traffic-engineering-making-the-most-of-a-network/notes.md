Assign children 5, 6, 7 (zero-based) to the detour under one prefix for 6–7 and one child prefix for 5, retaining the covering entry to the direct route.

[Lecture 10, lines 15870–15917](../../assets/f26-subtitles.txt#L15870) discusses the student question about one burst, averaging, and peak demand. The same-cycle thought experiment and timestamp suggestions are new.
- [Lecture 9, lines 14695–15391](../../assets/f26-subtitles.txt#L14695) and [Lecture 10, lines 15667–16058](../../assets/f26-subtitles.txt#L15667) derive edge–commodity variables, flow conservation, utilization scaling, and the student question about coupling between demands. The diagnostic u greater than one, the omitted third path challenge, and the plotted one-unit variant are new. No actual InstaPoll content is preserved at the end of Lecture 9.

[Lines 15396–15521](../../assets/f26-subtitles.txt#L15396) give controller isolation and separate logical or physical management networks. The specific utilization policy and anecdotes about deployments are omitted. The transient forwarding loop when updates differ in time is a new example.
